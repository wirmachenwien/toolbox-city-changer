# Toolbox for City Changers

The "Toolbox for City Changers" is for people who want to make their neighbourhood safer, greener, more inclusive and more resilient to climate change, and need practical tools to do so.

[Open the handbook](../book/en/0-3-contents.html){:.button}

**This handbook guides your initiative from an idea for fairer streets to concrete change**: from a clear concept (superblocks) and methods to make your vision visible (playful interventions), through tools that help residents see things differently (street design tools) and get involved, to evidence supporting your cause (monitoring) and accessible methods for local change (tactical urbanism).

"Toolbox for City Changers" is a collaboration between WirMachenWien, Changing Cities from Berlin and Prostorož from Ljubljana.

## Background

The content is based on how-to articles and workshop materials from [WirMachenWien](https://wirmachen.wien), supplemented by resources from partner organisations [Changing Cities](https://changing-cities.org) and [Prostorož](https://www.prostoroz.org).
